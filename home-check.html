FAQ

Kundli (also known as Janam Kundli or Birth Chart) is a detailed astrological chart prepared based on the exact date, time, and place of birth. It maps the positions of planets and celestial bodies at the time of your birth and is used to analyze various aspects of your life including personality, career, health, and relationships.

Our Kundli generation system uses precise astronomical calculations based on Swiss Ephemeris data. When you provide accurate birth details including the exact time and place, the generated Kundli is highly accurate and matches manually prepared charts by expert astrologers.

To generate an accurate Kundli, you need three key pieces of information: your exact date of birth, your exact time of birth (preferably from a birth certificate), and your place of birth. The more precise your birth time, the more accurate your Kundli will be.

In Vedic astrology, Kundli Milan (also called Guna Milan) is a traditional practice to assess compatibility between prospective partners. It analyzes 36 Gunas (qualities) and checks for Mangal Dosha. While it is a valuable tool for understanding compatibility, it should be considered as guidance rather than the sole deciding factor.

You can consult with our experienced astrologers by booking a session through our website. We offer both online (video call) and in-person consultations. Our team of scholars, established since 1974, provides authentic and detailed astrological guidance.
